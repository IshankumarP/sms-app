📲 Bulk SMS Sender

Send promotional messages from your own phone number.

Before You Start — One-time Phone Setup

1
Install SMS Gateway for Android on the phone you want to send from. Search for it on the Play Store, or find it at sms-gate.app.
2
Open the app. Tap the toggle to switch from Local to Cloud mode. The app will create an account and show you a username and password.
3
Note down those credentials — you'll enter them below each time you send.
4
Keep the app open in the background and your phone connected to the internet (WiFi or mobile data) while sending. If the phone screen locks, that's fine — just don't force-close the app.
⚠️ Important — Read Before Sending
  • Messages are sent from your SIM card. Replies will come to your phone.
  • Most carrier plans allow 100 SMS per day. The limit below is set to 100 — do not increase it without checking your plan.
  • Sending too fast can get your SIM flagged or temporarily blocked by your carrier. The app waits 3 seconds between each message automatically.
  • Only send to people who have agreed to receive promotional messages from you. Unsolicited bulk SMS is illegal in many regions.
  • Keep your phone plugged in during large sends — it may take several minutes.
  • Standard SMS is 160 characters max. Longer messages may be split and count as 2+ SMS against your daily limit.
